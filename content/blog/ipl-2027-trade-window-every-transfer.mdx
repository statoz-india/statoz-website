Fourteen players have changed franchises in the IPL 2027 trade window so far. The headline move is a direct swap that sends Hardik Pandya from Mumbai Indians to Punjab Kings and Arshdeep Singh the other way, with each player valued at ₹18 crore. Rishabh Pant has also returned to Delhi Capitals, and Sunrisers Hyderabad have been the busiest side in the market. Here is every deal reported up to the 17 November retention deadline, ahead of the mini-auction in Goa on 16 December 2026.

## Hardik Pandya and Arshdeep Singh swap sides

Mumbai Indians and Punjab Kings completed the biggest trade of the window: a straight player-for-player swap of Hardik Pandya and Arshdeep Singh, with both valued at ₹18 crore. Reports say Hardik's fee went up from ₹16.35 crore as part of the deal. Punjab get an all-rounder who can bat in the middle order and bowl seam, while Mumbai add a left-arm quick who bowls with the new ball and at the death. Early in November the move was still described as unconfirmed, but it was listed as completed before the deadline.

## Rishabh Pant goes home to Delhi

Rishabh Pant has left Lucknow Super Giants and returned to Delhi Capitals for ₹15 crore, well below the record ₹27 crore Lucknow paid for him at the 2025 auction. Kuldeep Yadav goes the other way to Lucknow for ₹13.5 crore. The swap was one of the first deals of the window, and it puts Pant back at the franchise where he began his IPL career.

## Four players switch between CSK and SRH

Chennai Super Kings and Sunrisers Hyderabad agreed a four-player deal in early November. Liam Livingstone (₹6 crore, revised down from ₹13 crore) and Jack Edwards (₹3 crore) move from Hyderabad to Chennai. Khaleel Ahmed (₹4.8 crore) and Nathan Ellis (₹2 crore) head from Chennai to Hyderabad. Chennai add power-hitting and all-round depth, while Hyderabad strengthen their pace attack with a left-armer and a death-overs specialist.

## T. Natarajan returns to Sunrisers Hyderabad

In mid-October, Delhi Capitals sent left-arm seamer T. Natarajan back to Sunrisers Hyderabad for ₹5 crore. Delhi had paid ₹10.75 crore for him at the 2025 auction. Leg-spinner Zeeshan Ansari moves from Hyderabad to Delhi for ₹40 lakh in return.

> Sunrisers Hyderabad have been involved in seven of the fourteen moves, more than any other franchise.

## Mohammed Shami and Sam Curran swap in a late deal

Just before the deadline, Lucknow Super Giants and Rajasthan Royals were reported to have agreed a three-player trade. Mohammed Shami (₹8 crore) and Tamil Nadu left-arm spinner Manimaran Siddharth (₹75 lakh) move from Lucknow to Rajasthan, while England all-rounder Sam Curran (₹2.4 crore) joins Lucknow. The deal reportedly frees up purse money for Lucknow, who had signed Shami for ₹10 crore.

## Harshal Patel joins Gujarat Titans for cash

Harshal Patel is reported to be moving from Sunrisers Hyderabad to Gujarat Titans in a ₹4 crore all-cash trade, the only deal of the window without a player going the other way. That is half the ₹8 crore Hyderabad paid for the two-time Purple Cap winner at the 2025 mega auction. Gujarat get a medium-pacer known for his slower balls, and a Gujarat-born player comes home.

## Key dates before IPL 2027

- **17 November 2026:** retention and release deadline, moved from 15 November to avoid a clash with an India vs New Zealand ODI.
- **23 November 2026:** player registration for the auction closes.
- **Around 9 December 2026:** the trade window pauses, about a week before the auction.
- **16 December 2026:** IPL 2027 mini-auction in Goa. The trade window reopens afterwards, so more moves are still possible before the season.

## Remaining purse for every team

After the trades and the 17 November retentions, the 10 franchises spent ₹1,002.05 crore on their squads and take a combined ₹297.95 crore into the auction. There are 66 squad places to fill, 25 of them for overseas players. Lucknow Super Giants have the most money to spend, while Gujarat Titans, who kept 22 players, have the least.
